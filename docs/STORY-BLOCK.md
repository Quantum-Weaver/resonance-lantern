# Resonance Lantern — Story Block

*Required for every room, atom, protocol, and surface in the Sanctuary, per
the Story Block Standard.*

## WHAT
A digital *camera lucida*: pick a reference image and it floats ghost-light,
opacity to your comfort, over the live camera pointed at your real paper.
Trace what you see through it — the way artists have learned for two
hundred years, rebuilt as a sovereign app. (`README.md`)

## HOW
Rebuilt on Tauri v2 + SvelteKit + Svelte 5 + Tailwind 4 + SQLite, cloned from
Resonance Echoes v1.1.0 (Echoes itself unaltered). (`README.md`)

## WHERE
The Sanctuary ecosystem — the Lantern Room, "the place you learn, step by
step, light in hand." Third of the original triad: Echoes looks back,
Compass looks around, Lantern looks forward.

## WHEN
Predecessor **CanvasGuide** built by the Weaver in four days (exact date not
recorded in this repo). Named **Resonance Lantern** by the Council,
2026-07-03 — the same sitting the rebuild-vs-Expo question was decided.

## WHY
"Tracing is learning, not cheating" — the way artists have learned for two
hundred years, made sovereign: no accounts, no tracking, local-first.
(`README.md`, `PRIVACY.md`)
